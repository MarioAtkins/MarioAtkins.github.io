Privacy Policy

What the site collects, what the service providers handle, and why.

This policy explains information collected through marioatkins.github.io and information you choose to provide when you schedule, request, or complete a service through linked providers.

Effective: September 23, 2026Last updated: September 23, 2026Florida, United States

1. Scope

This policy applies to the public website operated by Mario Atkins under The Florida Notary / The Florida Notaire brand and to information received directly in connection with service requests. Third-party platforms such as Cal.com and Proof maintain their own privacy practices for information processed on their systems.

Privacy questions: FloridaNotaire@gmail.com. Customer support website: marioatkins.github.io/notary.html.

2. Information you provide directly

Depending on the service, you may provide information such as:

  • Name, email address, telephone number, and preferred contact method.
  • Scheduling information, appointment details, service type, and answers to booking or intake questions.
  • Transaction or assignment information supplied by you, a title/settlement professional, law firm, government office, business, or other hiring party.
  • Feedback or review text you choose to send through the website’s review feature.
  • Documents or identification information you intentionally provide through an approved secure platform or transaction workflow.

The public website itself is not intended to collect government-issued identification or full transaction documents through ordinary page browsing.

3. Scheduling and intake through Cal.com

When you schedule through Cal.com, the booking system may process your name, contact information, time zone, appointment details, answers to booking questions, and information needed to coordinate the appointment. Cal.com states that, for bookings made through a user’s Cal.com account, the account holder generally determines the booking purposes while Cal.com processes attendee data on the account holder’s behalf.

Cal.com privacy information: cal.com/privacy.

4. RON and document information through Proof

Remote Online Notarization is performed through Proof, home of Notarize®. Proof may process information required to operate the notarization workflow, including contact details, government-issued identification, identity-verification information, transaction details, documents, and audio-video recordings of the notarial session.

Florida law requires online-notarization journal entries and audio-video recordings to be maintained for at least 10 years. Those statutory records are different from ordinary website analytics and may be retained through the RON platform or another lawful repository.

Proof privacy information: proof.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Website analytics

GoatCounter

We use GoatCounter for lightweight traffic analytics, such as page views and general browser or screen information. GoatCounter states that its hosted analytics service does not store visitor IP addresses, full user-agent strings, tracker IDs, or browser cookies for ordinary page counting.

GoatCounter privacy information: goatcounter.com/help/privacy.

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site and where the experience may be confusing. Clarity can capture interaction data such as clicks, scrolling, mouse movement, page rendering, and session-replay/heatmap information. Microsoft states that privacy-sensitive fields are masked by default, but Clarity may use first- and third-party cookies and pseudonymous identifiers for analytics.

Microsoft Clarity privacy information: clarity.microsoft.com/privacy. Microsoft Privacy Statement: microsoft.com/privacy/privacystatement.

6. How information is used

  • Schedule and coordinate appointments or assignments.
  • Prepare and perform the requested notarial or professional service.
  • Communicate about missing information, identity requirements, witnesses, document routing, completion, or customer support.
  • Maintain records required by law or reasonably necessary for business, security, audit, and dispute-resolution purposes.
  • Understand website traffic and improve page usability, navigation, and service instructions.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, security incidents, or unauthorized access.

7. When information may be shared

Information may be shared only as reasonably needed with:

  • Service providers used to host, schedule, communicate, analyze traffic, process a notarization, or deliver a completed workflow.
  • A hiring party, attorney, title/settlement professional, government office, or other participant when you or the assignment requires that information to complete the service.
  • Government agencies, courts, law enforcement, regulators, or other persons when disclosure is required or authorized by applicable law, subpoena, court order, notarial-record request, or lawful investigation.

We do not intentionally sell personal information for money or operate the website to build and sell advertising profiles. Third-party providers process data under their own published terms and privacy notices.

8. Cookies and browser controls

GitHub Pages, Microsoft Clarity, or other linked services may use cookies or similar technologies. Clarity’s ordinary deployment can set analytics cookies and pseudonymous identifiers. You can use browser controls to restrict or delete cookies, though doing so may affect how some third-party services function.

Because privacy and consent requirements vary by location, this policy describes the technologies currently present on the site but should not be read as a claim that a privacy notice alone satisfies every jurisdiction’s consent requirement.

9. Data retention

We keep ordinary business information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, to maintain records, resolve disputes, meet professional or contractual obligations, and comply with law. Retention periods can differ by information type and provider.

Online-notarization journal entries and audio-video recordings are subject to Florida’s statutory retention period, which is generally at least 10 years after the notarial act.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical steps appropriate to the service, including directing sensitive notarization documents and identity information to secure third-party workflows rather than ordinary public website forms. No internet transmission, email system, or third-party platform can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask what directly held information we have about you, request a correction, or request deletion where deletion is legally and operationally permitted. Some notarial records cannot be deleted on request because Florida law requires retention. Information controlled by Cal.com, Proof, Microsoft, GoatCounter, GitHub, or another provider may also need to be addressed through that provider’s privacy process.

12. External links

The site contains links to third-party websites and services. Their privacy practices are not controlled by this policy. Review the privacy notice presented by the third party before submitting sensitive information.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, providers, analytics tools, service workflows, or legal requirements change. The current version will remain posted at this URL with the effective date shown above.
