The law, for context.

Florida Statute 655.94 Requires the box to be opened in the presence of an officer and a notary public, who seals the contents and executes a certificate listing them. No oath, affidavit, or jurat is required, and no specific form is prescribed.
Florida Statute 117.107(12) A notary may not notarize if they have a financial interest in, or are a party to, the underlying transaction. An employee notarizing for their employer is not automatically disqualifying, absent a benefit beyond salary and the statutory fee.
National Notary Association Guidance Because Florida law provides no form certificate for this procedure, the NNA's Florida Notary Primer publishes a suggested format: present, inventory, certify, seal, without an oath.
