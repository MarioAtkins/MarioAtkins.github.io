The Nolan Wells Case

A Sourced Timeline and Open Questions — Ocean Springs, Mississippi, July 2026

How to read this page: Every timeline entry is tagged and sourced. "Confirmed" means reported directly by a named news outlet or official, with a link. "Family/attorney account" means it comes from Nolan's parents or attorney Ben Crump, on the record, but it is unclear whether it has been independently verified by investigators. "No news corroboration found" means a claim traces only to social media and no named outlet has confirmed it. Nothing here accuses a named individual of a crime; the questions ask what evidence exists to resolve conflicting accounts.
