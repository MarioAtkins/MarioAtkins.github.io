Case Work

Selected investigations.

Fraud Prevention Framework · C-Suite Submission · Wire Transfer Authentication
Remote Wire Authorization Gap: Proposed Industry Solution

Identified a structural authentication failure in the remote wire transfer process: the existing verification chain of online banking credentials, KBA questions, and document upload could be bypassed by anyone with access to a member's personal information. That population is precisely who commits wire fraud most often.

Designed and formally submitted a framework embedding Remote Online Notarization into wire authorization above a defined dollar threshold. The solution added credential analysis, deepfake detection, live recorded identity verification, a sworn jurat, and a bonded recovery mechanism, all without requiring branch presence. Presented to CEO, COO, and VP leadership.

The wire fraud gap this framework was designed to close still exists across the industry.
