4. Remote Online Notarization

Remote Online Notarization (“RON”) is performed through a third-party RON platform, currently Proof, home of Notarize®. RON requires real-time audio-video communication and identity-verification steps. The platform may require credential analysis, identity questions, government-issued identification, or other verification before a session can proceed.

Florida law requires an electronic journal and an audio-video record for online notarizations and generally requires those records to be retained for at least 10 years. Those records may be maintained through the RON platform or another lawful repository.

By choosing RON, you understand that the live notarial session is recorded as required by applicable law and platform procedures.
