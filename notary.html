Questions

Before you book

Can I complete the notarization online?

Many documents can be notarized remotely, but acceptance and document-specific requirements can vary. If the receiving party has given you instructions, follow those instructions and share them when you upload the document.

Can the signer be outside Florida?

For an eligible Florida RON transaction, the signer may be elsewhere while Mario remains physically located in Florida for the notarial act, subject to applicable requirements and receiving-party acceptance.

Can I jump directly into Proof?

Yes. Scheduling and uploading first is preferred because the document can be prepared before you join the live session. The direct signer-driven route is still available when needed.

What if I am not sure which option I need?

Call or text 321-537-2876. You do not need to solve the workflow before reaching out.
