Before You Book

What you'll need

Identification

You'll need valid, government-issued photo ID that satisfies Florida's identity verification requirements. No acceptable ID means the notarization can't happen.

Not sure if your ID qualifies? Call or text before you schedule, not after.

Your Document

Bring or upload the document you need notarized, ready to sign. Mario doesn't choose your document, draft it, or advise on its legal effect. If you have questions about what the document does, ask the attorney, lender, title company, or agency that gave it to you.

Need witnesses? You may provide your own eligible witness(es). If witnesses must be arranged for you, an additional fee may apply.

Questions

Frequently Asked

What is Remote Online Notarization?

A live, video-based notarization session that meets Florida's legal requirements for identity verification and witnessing, completed from your phone or computer instead of in person.

How much does online notarization cost?

$25 for your first notarization. Additional notarizations in the same session cost less than starting over, see the pricing table above.

What does "same session" mean?

It means documents notarized back to back in one sitting. Once that session ends, a later notarization, even the next day, is a brand new session at full starting price. Unused capacity from a package doesn't carry over.

What identification do I need?

Valid, government-issued photo ID that satisfies Florida's identity verification process. If you're unsure whether yours qualifies, contact Mario before scheduling.

Can I have more than one document notarized?

Yes. Multiple documents can be handled in one continuous session, and package pricing may apply.

What if I need witnesses?

You can provide your own eligible witnesses. If witnesses need to be arranged for you, an additional fee may apply.

Can you tell me which document I need?

No. Mario can notarize eligible, prepared documents, but can't provide legal advice or choose a legal document for you.

Can I schedule an in-person appointment?

Yes, local mobile appointments are available in Palm Bay and Melbourne. Use the "Schedule In Person" button above.

I already spoke with Mario. Do I need to schedule again?

Only if he specifically told you to proceed directly. See "Already Working with Mario" near the bottom of this page.

I represent a title company, law office, or business.

Scope

What I Can and Can't Do

Mario is a notary, not your attorney.

He Can
  • Verify your identity
  • Witness and perform authorized notarial acts
  • Administer oaths or affirmations where appropriate
  • Complete the correct notarial certificate when legally permitted
  • Maintain required records
He Can't
  • Give legal advice
  • Tell you whether to sign a document
  • Explain the legal consequences of your document
  • Choose a legal document for you
  • Draft legal documents
