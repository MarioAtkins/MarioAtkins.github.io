Secure Online Notarization

Proof handles the secure transaction environment

Proof protects transaction data using encryption in transit and at rest and provides identity-verification tools for the online workflow. Completed notarized documents are digitally signed and tamper-evident.

Florida RON also creates an electronic journal and audio-video record of the notarial session, retained as required by Florida law.

Payment

Expect a Stripe payment link before the session ends

Payment for Mario's notarial service is handled separately from Proof, home of Notarize®. After your document is uploaded and the required notarizations can be reviewed, Mario may send a Stripe payment link by text or email before or during the transaction.

Stripe checkout may offer credit or debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and ACH when available. If the actual number of notarizations or arranged witnesses changes during the live session, the final amount may be adjusted before completion.

For general-public transactions, payment is generally expected before Mario ends the completed session. Once the notarial work is complete and payment is confirmed, the completed electronic document is released through the Proof transaction workflow.

Questions

Frequently Asked

What is Remote Online Notarization?

A live audio-video notarization completed through an authorized online workflow while Mario, the Florida online notary, is physically located in Florida.

Can my signer be outside Florida?

Yes for an eligible Florida RON transaction. The signer may be elsewhere, subject to Florida law, Proof requirements, and the receiving party's acceptance of RON.

What if I only need an oath or affirmation?

Contact Mario directly. Remote administration may be available where legally permitted. Call or text 321-537-2876 or email FloridaNotaire@gmail.com.

Can you tell me which document I need?

No. Mario can notarize eligible prepared documents, but cannot choose a legal document or provide legal advice.
