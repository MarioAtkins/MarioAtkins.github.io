Fast Answers

What You Need to Know

What do you provide?

Remote Online Notarization, remote administration of oaths and affirmations, and local mobile notary service.

Why would an agency use it?

To avoid unnecessary travel, printing, and document routing when a signer or witness isn't in the same building — or the same state.

How fast?

Most sessions complete in one live meeting; the completed document can be available electronically shortly after, subject to payment and platform completion.

Can the signer be elsewhere?

Yes — anywhere in Florida, and in most cases outside Florida, subject to the receiving party's requirements.

What devices work?

Smartphone, tablet, laptop, or desktop — camera, microphone, internet, and a supported browser.

How do we start?

Upload the complete document securely through Proof and complete the guided preparation and identity-verification steps before meeting Mario.

How is the transaction protected?

Proof uses encrypted data transmission and storage, layered identity-verification technology, and digitally signed, tamper-evident notarized documents. Florida RON also creates an electronic journal and audio-video record of the notarial session.

What credentials support this?

Florida Notary and Online Notary commission, RON training, bond, and E&O insurance — see below.

Public-Sector Use

When This Helps

  • An employee or contractor is in another city or state
  • A parent, constituent, or vendor can't conveniently appear in person

  • Your internal notary is unavailable, off-duty, or restricted by policy or scheduling

The Details

Technology & Records

Remote Witnesses & Multiple Participants

A single live meeting supports up to 10 connected devices total, including the notary and all participants. Up to 2 witnesses may participate in an eligible meeting. Signers may provide their own eligible witnesses, or arrange one through the platform for an additional fee.

Device Requirements

A compatible smartphone, tablet, laptop, or desktop computer with a stable internet connection, camera, microphone, and supported browser. Most transactions require no app download.

Document Return

Once an eligible transaction is complete and any applicable payment is satisfied, the completed document can become available electronically. Actual availability and timing depend on the transaction and platform completion; no specific completion time is guaranteed.

Interstate Use

A Florida Online Notary Public physically located in Florida may perform an online notarization for a signer located elsewhere, subject to applicable law. This does not mean every recipient will accept a remotely notarized document — confirm acceptance with the receiving party before relying on RON for a specific matter.

Identity & Security Technology

Sessions are conducted through Proof's online notarization platform, which protects transaction data using encryption in transit and at rest, and advertises layered identity-verification and fraud-risk tools, including credential analysis, identity proofing, biometric comparison, and deepfake-indicator detection. These are risk-reduction tools, not guarantees.

Records & Evidentiary Value

Florida RON creates an electronic journal and audio-video record of every online notarization, retained for at least 10 years. A party to the electronic record has rights under Florida law to obtain pertinent journal information and a copy of the recording. These records do not determine the outcome of a dispute or guarantee that fraud did not occur.

Professional Safeguards

$100,000 Notary Errors & Omissions insurance. $25,000 Florida online notary bond. These are professional safeguards — they do not necessarily satisfy every insurance requirement a specific agency or contract may impose.
