Reduce coordination

One request. One Florida notary. Fewer handoffs.

The useful question is not only what the notarial act costs. It is how much time and coordination the whole transaction takes.

Remote where it works

Eligible signers can join remotely, reducing unnecessary travel, mileage, parking, printing, scanning, routing, and schedule friction.

Physical when needed

Local face-to-face service remains available in Palm Bay and Melbourne when wet ink or physical presence is required.

Repeat workflow support

When your organization uses the same document repeatedly, the operational setup can be mapped so each request does not start from zero.

Fewer handoffs

The document can move without sending everyone across town.

When RON fits the document and receiving-party requirements, the signer can meet remotely while your team keeps one Florida notary contact. When physical presence is needed, the local option stays available.
