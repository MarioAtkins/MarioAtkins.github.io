One Request, One Florida Notary

Keep the assignment moving even when the participants are not in the same place

A signer can be in another office, another Florida city, or another state for an eligible RON transaction. Your team keeps one Florida-based notary contact instead of sourcing a new local notary wherever the signer happens to be.

That can reduce coordination, travel, document routing, and repeat handoffs.

Total Transaction Cost

The notarial fee is only one part of the workflow

RON can reduce employee travel, mileage, parking, tolls, printing, scanning, postage, courier expense, physical document routing, scheduling friction, and time away from assigned duties.

RON is not represented as the lowest-cost option in every circumstance. The useful comparison is the complete transaction workflow.

Security & Records

Secure online notarization through Proof

Proof protects transaction data using encryption in transit and at rest and provides layered identity-verification and fraud-risk tools. Completed notarized documents are digitally signed and tamper-evident.

Florida RON creates an electronic journal and audio-video record retained for at least 10 years. These records add documentation to the notarial process if a transaction is later questioned.

How It Works

A short online workflow

  1. Upload. Upload the complete document securely through Proof.
  2. Prepare. Provide the required signer information and review the document.
  3. Verify. The signer completes Proof's identity-verification process.
  4. Meet. Join Mario by live audio-video to complete the notarization.
  5. Receive. The completed electronic document becomes available after completion and applicable payment requirements.

Scope

Notarial service, clearly bounded

Mario is a notary, not an attorney, title agent, court reporter, or document preparer.

Mario Can
  • Perform authorized notarial acts
  • Administer authorized oaths and affirmations
  • Verify identity under the applicable notarial workflow
  • Maintain required RON records
  • Complete the notarial certificate when legally permitted
Mario Does Not
  • Provide legal advice or representation
  • Select or draft legal documents
  • Conduct depositions or provide court reporting
  • Provide expert-witness testimony
  • Guarantee that a receiving party will accept RON
