Important boundary

The court provides the form. You control the legal decision.

This independent service does not determine whether a form is appropriate, what information belongs in it, whether it should be filed, or what legal effect it may have.

  • Use the official Florida Courts instructions for the form you select.
  • For legal advice or help deciding what to file, consult an attorney or an appropriate court self-help resource.
  • Notarization does not validate the legal content of a document.
  • Receiving-party, filing, witness, and document-specific requirements can vary.
