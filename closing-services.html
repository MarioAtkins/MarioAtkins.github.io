Questions

Professional Assignment FAQ

Can I send the same document format to Mario repeatedly?

Yes. If your organization repeatedly supplies the same document format, a prior transaction setup may be reusable where appropriate. Your organization still supplies or confirms the applicable document. Mario does not choose a legal form for the signer.

Can Mario fix a closing error after the signer leaves?

Potentially. If your team supplies the corrected document or instructions and the transaction is eligible for RON, Mario may be able to perform the required notarial act remotely. The responsible title company, lender, attorney, or document issuer determines what correction is required.

Can a signer be outside Brevard County?

Yes for an eligible RON transaction. Mario performs the online notarial act while physically located in Florida. The transaction must still satisfy applicable Florida law and the receiving party's requirements.

Do you handle physical signing appointments?

Yes. Local professional mobile assignments are available when wet ink or physical presence is required or preferred. Pricing is quoted based on the assignment scope, travel, printing, scan-back, shipping, and other known requirements.

Are you available after normal business hours?

Remote assignments are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, subject to availability. For an urgent closing or correction, call or text Mario directly.
